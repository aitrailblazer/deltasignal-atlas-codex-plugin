Fund Usage After the Free Calls

The first 5 calls are free. Paid calls use USDC on Base through x402 micropayments.

Wallet

Use a Base-Compatible Wallet

Coinbase Wallet, Coinbase Smart Wallet, or another Base-compatible wallet works. Switch the wallet network to Base.

USDC

Add Base USDC

Start with $1-$5 for testing. Buy USDC on Coinbase and send it to your wallet on Base.

Settle

Approve x402 Payments

When the agent receives a 402 payment challenge, approve it or let your x402-compatible client settle the request.
